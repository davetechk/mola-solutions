Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

MOLA Solutions operates an internet workspace and gaming cafe. This policy explains how we handle your personal information in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Information we collect

  • Your name and phone number when you register or contact us.
  • Payment details such as transfer reference. We do not store card numbers.
  • Basic session records, including time used and the workstation or console assigned.
  • Messages you send to us by phone, WhatsApp or email.

How we use it

  • To provide and time your sessions and process payments.
  • To answer your questions and handle complaints or refund requests.
  • To keep our equipment and network secure.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Your browsing

We do not read your private messages or save your passwords. Please log out of your accounts and close all browser sessions before leaving a workstation. We are not responsible for information left on shared computers.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with payment providers or authorities when required by law.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us through the Contact page.

Retention and changes

We keep records only as long as needed for the purposes above. We may update this policy and will post the new date at the top of this page.
