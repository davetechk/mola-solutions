Common questions

Do I need to book?

No. Walk in and take any free workstation. For a PS4 or PS5 on busy days, contact us first to reserve.

Can I bring my own laptop?

Yes. You can use our internet on your own device and pay for the time you use.

How do I pay?

Pay at the counter before or at the start of your session. Time begins when you log in.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, in some cases such as faulty equipment or long internet outages. Read our Refund Policy.
