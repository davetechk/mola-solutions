Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

This policy explains when MOLA Solutions will refund or credit payments for internet, workspace and gaming time.

When we refund

  • A workstation or console is faulty and we cannot move you to a working one.
  • The internet is unavailable for a long period during your paid session. We will refund or credit the lost time.
  • You were charged more than the listed price.

When we do not refund

  • Time already used.
  • Sessions ended early because you chose to leave.
  • Sessions ended because of a breach of our Terms of Use.
  • Loss of work or data caused by your own device or account.

How to request a refund

Tell a staff member at the counter before you leave, or contact us within 24 hours of your visit using the details on our Contact page. Approved refunds are paid in cash or by transfer within 3 working days.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.
